Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Court Consulting LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), operator of the Kinly service ("Kinly," the "Service"), collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our availability-first babysitter scheduling service.

By using Kinly or sending a text message to our number, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Who we are

Kinly is a scheduling service that helps families coordinate with babysitters they already know and trust. Babysitters share their availability (primarily by text message); parents see which of their connected sitters are available and send booking requests.

The Service is operated by Court Consulting LLC, located at 217 Sylvan St, Rutherford, NJ 07070. You can reach us at bethbuehler@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the scheduling service:

  • Contact information: phone numbers of sitters and parents, and names where provided.
  • Availability information: the dates, times, and notes that sitters submit (for example, "free Saturday 6–10pm, can drive").
  • Family information: parent-provided details needed for a booking, which may include a child's first name and care instructions (for example, allergies or bedtime).
  • Booking information: records of requests, confirmations, declines, and cancellations.
  • Message content: the text messages you send to and receive from the Service, used to operate the availability and booking features.

We do not collect payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, or government identifiers through the Service.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the scheduling service — record availability, match sitters to a parent's requested window, and manage booking requests, confirmations, and cancellations.
  • Send transactional text messages related to availability and bookings (for example, confirming a posted window, notifying a sitter of a request, or notifying a parent of a confirmation).
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for third-party advertising. We do not share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Text messaging

By providing your phone number or texting our number, you consent to receive text messages from Kinly related to scheduling and bookings. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your activity.

  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe and stop receiving messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at bethbuehler@gmail.com.

Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase. See our Terms of Service for more detail on how consent works.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Between connected users. A sitter's availability is visible to the parents she is connected to; a parent's booking details (including the relevant child's name and care instructions for that booking) are shared with the sitter for that booking.
  • Service providers. We use trusted vendors to operate the Service — for example, a messaging provider to send and receive texts and a database provider to store information. They may process information only on our behalf and under obligations of confidentiality.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.

We do not otherwise disclose your information to third parties.

Children's information

Kinly is intended for use by adults (parents and babysitters). Accounts are not created by or for children. In the course of arranging care, a parent may provide limited information about their child (such as a first name and care instructions) so that a sitter can care for that child. We use this information solely to facilitate the requested booking and do not use it for any other purpose. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

If you believe a child has provided us information directly, please contact us at bethbuehler@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate business or legal purposes. You may request deletion of your information as described below.

Your choices and rights

  • Access or delete your information: contact us at bethbuehler@gmail.com and we will respond consistent with applicable law.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information under state privacy laws.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
