SMS Consent & Opt-In

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This page describes how users consent to receive text messages from Kinly, a service of Court Consulting LLC. It supplements our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What Kinly is

Kinly is an availability-first scheduling service that helps families coordinate with babysitters they already know and trust. The service operates primarily over SMS: sitters share their availability by text, and parents receive booking-related messages.

How users opt in

Consent to receive text messages from Kinly is obtained directly from each user before messages are sent.

Babysitters are personally invited to Kinly by a family they already work with. A sitter opts in by texting Kinly's phone number to share her availability. That first inbound message establishes her consent to receive scheduling-related texts (availability confirmations and booking requests).

Parents opt in through Kinly's registration process. During registration, a parent provides their mobile number and affirmatively agrees to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and consents to receive booking-related text messages. Because a parent may add care details about their child (such as a first name, allergies, or bedtime) to arrange a booking, the parent explicitly agrees to provide that information for the purpose of arranging care before entering it.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or of using the service.

What messages you'll receive

Messages are transactional and relate to scheduling activity you initiate or participate in, for example:

  • To a sitter: confirmation that her availability was posted; a request when a connected family wants to book her; and booking confirmations, declines, or cancellations.
  • To a parent: confirmation, decline, or cancellation of a booking she requested.

Sample messages:

  • Got it! You're free 6pm-10pm on Saturday. Your connected families can see you.
  • The Reyes want you 6pm-10pm Saturday for Luke. Reply YES to accept, NO to pass.
  • Confirm: 6pm-10pm Saturday with the Reyes? Reply Y to lock it in.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on your activity and the number of families or sitters you are connected to. Message and data rates may apply.

How to opt out or get help

  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. After you send STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and then no further messages, except as needed to process your opt-out.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at bethbuehler@gmail.com">bethbuehler@gmail.com.

Privacy

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full detail on how we handle your information, including information about children.
